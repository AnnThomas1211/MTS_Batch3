Reward Module: Eligibility & Rules

🟢 Successful Transfer

Transaction status must be SUCCESS to qualify for point allocation.

🟢 Amount ≥ $100

Only transactions of $100 or more are eligible to earn points.

🟢 Peer-to-Peer

Sender and receiver must be different accounts.

🟢 No Self-Transfers

Transfers between accounts owned by the same user are disqualified.

Calculation Logic

1 point for every $100 transferred, rounded down (using integer division). Integrated safely inside database transactions.

points = floor(Amount / 100)
Transfer of $250.00 +2 Points
Transfer of $99.00 0 Points (Amount < 100)
Transfer of $100 (Self) 0 Points (Disqualified)
